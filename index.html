About me

Hi, I’m Shruti. Welcome to my watercolor sketchbook.

I’m a forever lover of watercolor, but just a novice when it comes to producing anything professional. Every time I pick up the brush, I fall in love with watercolor all over again.

I created this site to document my entire adventure: the small breakthroughs, the unexpected messes, and every brushstroke in between as I mature as an artist. This space is an honest window into my creative journey.
